DATA SOURCES & METHODOLOGY

Forest change: Hansen/UMD Global Forest Change v1.13, Landsat-based, approximately 30 m spatial resolution.

Baseline: Tree canopy cover in 2000. The canopy threshold can be adjusted in the controls.

Annual loss: Loss-year raster values 1–25 represent 2001–2025. Zero indicates no detected loss during the observation period.

Area calculation: Sampled raster cells meeting the baseline canopy threshold and showing loss are converted to approximate hectares.

Important: Tree-cover loss is not automatically deforestation. It includes disturbance in natural forests, plantations and other tree-cover types, including fire and temporary loss. It does not subtract subsequent regrowth.

The sample-grid estimate is an approximation, not a complete count of every 30 m pixel. National statistics and selected-area estimates have different spatial aggregation methods.

Methodological changes in the Global Forest Change time series mean that comparisons across some years should be interpreted cautiously.
